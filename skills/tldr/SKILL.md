Long-form paragraphs are a heavy cognitive load to digest. Keep the substance, change the shape.

## Scope

Applies to one response only.

- `/tldr` — restructure the response just given. Do not drop detail or re-answer.
- `/tldr <question>` — answer the question in this format.

## Format

Keep responses direct and digestible. No long paragraphs. Write in ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English.

Prefer examples, visualisations, diagrams, and tables wherever they are relevant.

Group related information into sections. Use point form and inline formatting — bold, emphasis, code — to flag important keywords, concepts, and takeaways.
